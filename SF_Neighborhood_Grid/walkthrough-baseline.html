How this neighborhood layout was made

The CSV is grouped by Analysis Neighborhood, not Supervisor District. DataSF supplies 41 analysis areas; 40 have records in this CSV, while Visitacion Valley has none and stays visible in gray. The mainland grid follows a sampled city silhouette; areas are matched to cells by minimizing displacement from geographic representative points. True borders and every adjacency are not preserved. These analysis areas aggregate census tracts and are not legally defined neighborhood boundaries.
